Dear Mahi,

Happy birthday. ♡ I wish I could put every good memory, every laugh, every random little moment, and every thing I’m thankful for into this notebook—but I think we'd need about a thousand pages.

Thank you for being you. Thank you for the silly moments that become stories, for the times you listen, for the way you show up for people you care about, and for being a part of my life in a way that matters more than I probably say out loud.

I’m genuinely grateful for the memories we’ve made, even the completely unserious ones. The random reels, the recordings, the chaos, the moments when neither of us knows what we’re doing—somehow, those little things become the memories I want to keep.

I hope this year is gentle with you. I hope you feel appreciated without having to ask, supported when things are hard, and surrounded by people who notice the wonderful things about you. And when you forget how much you matter, I hope life gives you a hundred little reminders.

Thank you for being part of my story, Mahi. I’m rooting for you, always. Here’s to more memories, more laughter, more ridiculous videos, and more pages we haven’t written yet.

With all my love and gratitude,
Kushagra ♡
